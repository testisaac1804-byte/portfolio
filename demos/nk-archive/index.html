How this was made

The .kp nameservers sit inside the same thin international link the country uses for everything else, and that link is shut. Queries return SERVFAIL worldwide: No Reachable Authority at delegation kp. So the archive was rebuilt from the Internet Archive instead - a breadth-first crawl from each site's real front page, following its own links, not an arbitrary sample of URLs.

What is missing. Each site is capped at 45 pages - enough to carry the front page and its navigation, not the full back catalogue. 7 domains have no capture anywhere: cooksfriends.org.kp kiyctc.com.kp knic.com.kp korelcfund.org.kp ryongnamsan.edu.kp silibank.net.kp star-co.net.kp . Those were never crawled by anyone, so no archive can contain them.
Some pages render unstyled. Four sites (rodong, yongsaeng, kut.edu, fia.law) lost their stylesheets - the archive returns 404 for the CSS itself. The text is complete; the design was never captured. Verified against the origin, not assumed.
Why the count is honest. 1,255 pages is not 1,255 articles. Several of the biggest sites are document and periodical dumps - a scanned book library counts as thousands of pages. The wire services are the ones with real daily copy.
