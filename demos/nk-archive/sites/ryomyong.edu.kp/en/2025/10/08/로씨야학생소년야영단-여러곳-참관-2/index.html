Visit of Russian Young Campers to Different Places
2025.10.08.

The Russian young campers, who participated in DPRK-Russia juvenile friendship camping, visited Mangyongdae Schoolchildren’s Palace.

While looking around different circle rooms, they were briefed about the fact that the Korean schoolchildren grow up happily and brightly to be reliable pillars of future, bringing their talents and hopes into full bloom under the benefit of the socialist educational system.

They also visited the Tower of the Juche Idea, Liberation Tower and other places, and enjoyed themselves at Munsu Water Park, Kaeson Youth Park Fun Fair and other bases for cultural and leisure activities.
