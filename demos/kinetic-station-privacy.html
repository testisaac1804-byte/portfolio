Kinetic Industries — Privacy Policy

Last Updated: 1 January 2026

1. Information We Collect

When you register for a Kinetic Industries membership, we collect:

  • Identity Information: Name, email address, phone number, age, nationality.
  • Organisational Information: Company or school name, partner organisation.
  • Project Information: Project descriptions, industry category.
  • Payment Information: Payment method, transaction history, wallet balance.
  • Usage Data: Machine bookings, job history, session timers, door access logs.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used to:

  • Provide and manage your membership and access to toolheads.
  • Process wallet transactions and material dispensing.
  • Maintain booking records and prevent double-booking.
  • Generate analytics and usage statistics (anonymised where possible).
  • Communicate announcements, maintenance updates, and policy changes.

3. Data Storage

All member data is stored locally in your browser using localStorage. No data is transmitted to external servers except for the optional Formspree integration during account registration. You may export or delete your data at any time through the terminal.

4. Data Sharing

Kinetic Industries does not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties, except:

  • When required by law or legal process.
  • With your explicit consent.
  • Anonymised usage statistics may be shared for reporting purposes.

5. Data Security

We implement reasonable security measures to protect your data. However, as data is stored in your browser's localStorage, you are responsible for maintaining the security of your device and browser.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal data through the terminal dashboard.
  • Correct inaccurate data through the profile editor.
  • Export your data in JSON format through the "My Data" button.
  • Delete your account and associated data through the admin panel.

7. Cookies & Local Storage

This application uses browser localStorage to maintain your session, settings, and preferences. No tracking cookies or third-party analytics are used.

8. Contact

For privacy-related inquiries, please use the Enquiry Support system in the terminal or contact the studio administrator.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Members will be notified of material changes via the announcement system.
