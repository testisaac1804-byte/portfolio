A meme history of China, one year at a time

From a chat-room 886 in 2000 to 胆子真是肥嘟嘟的 in 2026: 250 memes, each with where it came from and what it was actually doing. Twenty-seven year sections, 27 long-form “meme of the year” pieces, five eras — inside one HTML file that loads nothing from the network.

The correction that set the shape

“oh i expected same thing for every year” — after a first version that only covered 2026, then “maybe add more memes for every year and also add 2000-2007”. The unit of the deliverable stopped being the meme and became the year: every year needed the same structure, not just the years with good material.

What shipped

1

27 year sections, identical skeleton

Each year opens with a numeral and a one-line note, then one tier-1 “meme of the year” card with a three-paragraph origin story plus two boxes (why it is this one, how to use it), then 5–11 short cards with a single origin line and one paragraph. 2000–2007 is thin by nature (6–9); 2009 onward holds 9–12.

2

Two artifacts, cross-linked

meme-history.html is the 27-year page. 2026-meme-history.html is the deep version of the current year — 47 entries arranged by month, with the long research on 胆子真是肥嘟嘟的, 牛来, MJ and the 《我的前半生》 rerun. The main page drops to 12 entries for 2026 and links out to it.

3

Search that understands a year

Full-text search over name, origin, story, why and use. A bare four-digit year takes a different branch and returns exactly that year's entries instead of every card that happens to mention the number. Tag pills filter by mechanism; both compose, and the count label reads 找到 N / 250.

4

Prints like a document

Paper palette, hard edges, no gradients on text. Cards avoid breaking across pages; the year numeral stays with its section. The whole thing is one file, so a printed copy and an emailed copy are the same bytes.

The data shape

Entry

A flat object: y (year), name, tag, tier, origin, story[]. Tier 1 adds why, use, src. No IDs, no relationships — the year is the only grouping, which is why a new year costs one array.

Eras

Five ranges with a paragraph each: 2000–2007 网络方言, 2008–2012 围观, 2013–2017 造词, 2018–2022 短视频, 2023–2026 抽象与情绪. They are an argument about how memes were made, not decoration — each one names the medium that produced them.

Search index

Built at render time: every field joined into one lowercased string on the card's dataset. 250 cards × ~150 characters is small enough that filtering is a loop, not an index — no library, no debounce needed.

Payload

One JSON blob (106,252 bytes) inside <script type="application/json">. Rendering is innerHTML from a template string, so the page has no build step, no framework and no hydration — open it from a USB stick and it works.

Five eras, and the claim each one makes

RangeEraThe argument
2000–2007网络方言时代Memes grew out of typing habits — 886, 斑竹, 灌水 — not out of news events. Cheap bandwidth, expensive minutes.
2008–2012围观时代Material came from news, the Spring Festival Gala and TV dramas; the job of a meme was to close a case on something.
2013–2017造词时代Users started manufacturing words (人艰不拆, 十动然拒) and compressing whole paragraphs into four characters.
2018–2022短视频时代The unit stopped being a word and became a clip and an audio track; lifespan fell from years to weeks.
2023–2026抽象与情绪时代AI zeroed the cost of二创, archaeology became mainstream, and memes stopped being funny first — they speak for people first.

Where the facts came from

2026 — researched per meme

胆子真是肥嘟嘟的, 命运你假糍粑 and 来杯好茶摇一摇 were searched individually before writing; the 2026 round-ups were read for the month-by-month frame. That pass produced the 47-entry detail page, which this page condenses to 12.

2024–2025 — checked against lists

Cross-checked with 《咬文嚼字》's annual top-ten (班味, 松弛感, 抽象 / 活人感, 赛博对账, 预制××, ××基础××不基础) and the 2025 state language-resource list (DeepSeek, 敬自己一杯, 如何呢又能怎).

2000–2007 — event-level sources

The eight kings rest on documented events: 馒头案, 华南虎照片, 木子美, 芙蓉姐姐, 2005 超女, 痞子蔡's novel. Each king links its Wikipedia entry on the card.

2008–2023 — historiography

These memes are heavily documented and the events are not in dispute; the wording, framing and the “why it is this one” judgement are mine. No entry quotes a figure I did not have a source for.

Dates are assigned to the year a meme spread widest, not the day it was born. 很黄很暴力 aired in December 2007 and is filed under 2008; 命运的齿轮 was already circulating in late 2022. Where the two years disagree, the year with the bigger wave wins — the timeline is about when a thing belonged to everyone.Filing rule, applied to all 250 entries

Honest limitations

2000–2007 is genuinely sparser. Six to nine entries a year, not ten or twelve. The era had fewer memes and thinner documentation — padding it to match 2015 would mean inventing items, so the shape of the page shows the shape of the record instead.
  • No images, by design. Adding a picture per year would break the zero-request property and the print fidelity. It is the one obvious upgrade left.
  • 2026 stops at August. September–October are still moving; the detail page says so rather than freezing a half-formed list.
  • Some 2026 items are contested. The MJ effect, for instance, is reported by media but never confirmed by the platform — the entry states both rather than picking a side.
  • Judgement calls are visible. “Meme of the year” is an argument, not a measurement. Each card carries a why it is this one box so the reasoning can be disagreed with.

Honesty notes. The 250 entries are not equally sourced: 2026 and 2024–2025 were checked against lists and searches, 2008–2023 rely on well-documented events with my own wording, and 2000–2007 lean on a handful of event pages. Dates follow the “widest spread” rule. Nothing here is a quote I could not attribute, and no entry was written to fill a slot.
