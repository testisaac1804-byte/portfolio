Multi-technology 3D printing slicer with 14 technologies — more than BambuStudio or OrcaSlicer. PyQt6 + OpenGL desktop app with Bambu Lab MQTT connectivity.

macOS .app Bundle

🎯 14 Technologies

FDM, SLA, DLP, MSLA, SLS, MJF, DMLS, SLM, PolyJet, Binder Jetting, CLIP, EBM, LOM, FGF — more than any other slicer.

🖨 10 Built-in Printers

All 4 Bambu Lab models (X1C, P1S, A1, A1 Mini), Ender 3, Prusa MK4, Elegoo Saturn, Anycubic Photon, Formlabs Form 3, Sinterit Lisa.

🔗 Bambu Lab MQTT

Full printer control via MQTT — start, pause, resume, stop, set temps, camera stream. LAN discovery with SSDP.

🎨 Modern UI

Dark theme (#0a0e1a / #e94560 accent), sidebar navigation, stacked settings pages, splash screen with loading animation.

👁 3D Viewer

OpenGL-rendered STL/OBJ/3MF viewer with orbit, pan, zoom. G-code toolpath preview with layer coloring.

⚙ 11 Settings Panels

Quality, Walls, Infill, Speed, Temperature, Support, Adhesion, Advanced — plus SLA/SLS/DMLS-specific settings.
