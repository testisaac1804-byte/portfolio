# Snake (Casio fx-CG50)

Classic Snake compiled as a `.g3a` add-in for the Casio fx-CG50 graphing calculator.

## What it is
- Grow the snake, eat the food, don't hit the walls or yourself
- Playable with the calculator's keypad
- `Downloads/casio-games/fx-CG50SNAKE.g3a`

## Built with
Casio fx-CG50 add-in toolchain (C), part of Isaac's calculator-game collection.
