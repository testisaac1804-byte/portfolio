# IGCSE Physics - Forces Revision Notes (build spec)

**Deliverable:** one self-contained single-file HTML revision guide at `notes/Forces-Double-Award-Notes.html`

**Board:** IGCSE Physics (Double Award).

## Content (40 sections)
Vectors and scalars; contact and non-contact forces; resultant forces; Newton's laws; mass vs weight; gravity and g; terminal velocity; Hooke's law; moments, levers and the principle of moments; centre of gravity and stability; pressure in solids, liquids and gases; upthrust and floating; drag and streamlining; stopping, thinking and braking distances; momentum; formulas summary with worked examples and a practice set.

## Build rules
Dark theme, self-contained HTML, formula boxes, worked examples, no external assets.
