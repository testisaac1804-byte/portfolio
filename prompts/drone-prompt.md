# Drone (Isaac design)

A custom quadcopter drone designed by Isaac — "drone isaac".

## What it is
- Original lightweight drone body design
- `Documents/drone/drone isaac.stl` (3D-print ready mesh)
- `Documents/drone/drone isaac.3mf` (print project — includes plate/print settings)

## Design notes
- Frame designed for a small quadcopter build (print in PLA/PETG)
- Modelled in Fusion 360, exported for the Bambu Lab printer
