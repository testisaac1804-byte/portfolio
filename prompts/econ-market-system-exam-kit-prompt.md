# Economics 1.1 Market System - Exam Kit (build spec)

**Deliverable:** one self-contained single-file HTML study guide at `reference/economics/Economics-1.1-Market-System-Exam-Kit.html`

**Board:** Pearson Edexcel International GCSE Economics (4EC1) - Paper 1, 1h30, 80 marks, 4 x 20-mark compulsory questions.

## Scope
1.1.1 The economic problem: scarcity, needs vs wants, opportunity cost, the four factors of production and their rewards (rent/wages/interest/profit), the three fundamental questions, and opportunity cost for consumers, workers, producers and government.
1.1.1c-e The PPF: definition, assumptions, the five things it shows (max productive potential, full employment, opportunity cost, economic growth, possible vs unobtainable), worked numerical example, outward/inward shift causes, movement-vs-shift table.
1.1.3 Demand and Supply: definitions, laws, determinants that shift each curve, movement vs shift, substitutes vs complements, fixed supply.
1.1.3g-j Market equilibrium: equilibrium price/quantity, market clearing price, excess demand and excess supply, worked tables (Uber rides, bicycle worksheet), excess calculations, and how shifts move the equilibrium.

## Exam-skills section (the point of the guide)
Paper structure, AO weightings, timing plan at ~1.1 min/mark. Command-word table (MCQ, Define, State, What is meant by, Describe, Calculate, Draw, Explain, Analyse, Assess, Evaluate) with marks, AO split and what earns each mark. Level descriptors for 6/9/12-mark questions and the rule that one viewpoint caps at Level 1. Full model answers at 2, 3, 6, 9 and 12 marks. Common-mistakes table. Diagram-mark checklist. Practice quiz with self-reveal answers. 5-day revision plan.

## Build rules
Dark theme, sticky tab navigation, hand-authored inline SVG diagrams (PPF, demand, demand shift, supply, equilibrium, excess demand/supply), checkbox checklist persisted to localStorage, vanilla JS only, no external assets.
