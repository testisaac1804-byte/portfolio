# Flappy Bird (Casio fx-CG50)

Flappy Bird recreated as a compiled `.g3a` add-in running on the Casio fx-CG50 graphing calculator.

## What it is
- Tap to flap, dodge the pipes, survive as long as you can
- Draws to the calculator's colour screen
- `Downloads/casio-games/Flappybi.g3a`

## Built with
Casio fx-CG50 add-in toolchain (C), part of Isaac's calculator-game collection.
